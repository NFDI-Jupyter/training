# Learning paths

The modules are independent, but the following sequences can help if you have a particular goal.

## I am new to Jupyter4NFDI

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md) — sign in, launch an environment, and explore JupyterLab.
2. [Programming languages and kernels](https://nfdi-jupyter.github.io/learn-jupyter4nfdi-languages-kernels/) — understand how notebooks execute code.
3. [Working with external data storage](https://nfdi-jupyter.github.io/learn-jupyter4nfdi-mouting-data/) — connect the data you need.

## I want to bring my research project to Jupyter4NFDI

1. [Prepare your project to be Jupyter4NFDI-ready](https://nfdi-jupyter.github.io/learn-jupyter4nfdi-ready/) — define dependencies and a reproducible environment.
2. [Programming languages and kernels](https://nfdi-jupyter.github.io/learn-jupyter4nfdi-languages-kernels/) — configure the kernels your work needs.
3. [Automated Notebook Validation](https://nfdi-jupyter.github.io/learn-jupyter4nfdi-automated-validation/) — check that notebooks continue to run.

## I need additional computing or data resources

1. [Working with external data storage](https://nfdi-jupyter.github.io/learn-jupyter4nfdi-mouting-data/) — connect data to your work.
2. [Getting access to GPUs](https://nfdi-jupyter.github.io/learn-jupyter4nfdi-gpu/) — explore GPU computing and learn how to request access.

The suggested orders are guides, not prerequisites. Pick the modules most relevant to your work.
